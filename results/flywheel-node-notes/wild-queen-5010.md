## Summary

Paper hover-family node covering HM1, HM2, and HM3. Reported results: HM1 improves FoM by 3.081% at theta75 = 7 deg; HM2 improves FoM by 1.988% at theta75 = 10 deg; HM3 improves FoM by 2.046% at theta75 = 10 deg with twist, chord, and sweep active. The repo codifies the same variable subsets and fixed-thrust objective for low-cost open reruns.

## Hypothesis

For hover design points at fixed thrust, twist optimization should improve figure of merit, while adding chord and sweep should produce only a small extra hover benefit.
