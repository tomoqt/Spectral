# Insight: Chord and sweep matter more in airplane mode than in hover

- slug: `delicate-sun-0460`
- kind: `insight`
- parents: `dry-hall-6882`, `wild-queen-5010`, `red-paper-3383`

## Summary

The paper's reported evidence says chord and sweep provide only marginal extra hover benefit but materially larger airplane-mode benefit, which the authors attribute to reduced tip compressibility penalties.

## Notes

- HM2 to HM3 changes hover FoM only from +1.988% to +2.046%.
- AM1 to AM2 changes airplane efficiency from +6.593% to +8.180%.
- This asymmetry is why the open model includes an explicit tip-Mach drag-rise term and a sweep-based reduction in effective normal Mach number.
