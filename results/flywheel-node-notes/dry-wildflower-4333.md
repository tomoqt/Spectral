## Summary

The paper's multi-point family does not search for a single universal optimum; it searches for compromise blades whose performance sits between hover-optimal and airplane-optimal single-point designs.

## Notes

- MP1 and MP2 keep twist-only design variables and differ only by their hover versus airplane weights.
- MP3 adds chord and sweep, reaching the paper's best compromise: -0.387% FoM with +4.945% airplane efficiency.
