## Summary

Paper airplane-family node covering AM1 and AM2. Reported results: AM1 improves propeller efficiency by 6.593%; AM2 improves it by 8.180% once chord and sweep are also active. The repo preserves the same design-variable subsets and cruise design point in the open model.

## Hypothesis

For the airplane design point at fixed thrust, twist optimization should improve propeller efficiency, and adding chord plus sweep should further improve efficiency by mitigating tip compressibility penalties.
